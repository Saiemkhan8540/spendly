Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Here is how Spendly handles your information.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide when you create an account, such as your name and email address, along with the expense details you log: amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to run Spendly: to show your expenses, build your summaries and category breakdowns, and keep your account secure. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in our database and protected with reasonable security measures, including hashed passwords. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when pages load. We do not share your expense data with third parties, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want your data removed, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
